FreeWealthCare: private, local-first budgeting

Give every dollar a job with a zero-based budget. Assign money to categories, track spending, and carry unspent balances into the next month.

Enter transactions manually or import a bank CSV file. No account or bank login is required. Your budget is stored in this browser on this device.

Export backups regularly. Clearing browser data can erase your budget, and backups are not automatically synced to another device.

FreeWealthCare is an educational budgeting tool, not financial, tax, or investment advice.
